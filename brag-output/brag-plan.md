# Brag Plan: Tadka (AI-Voice Copilot)

## What is this app?
An offline-first AI Voice Copilot and Revenue Engine that takes restaurant phone orders in English, Hindi, and Hinglish with zero cloud API costs, data-backed upselling, and direct POS dispatch.

## The angle
Restaurants lose massive high-margin revenue on chaotic phone calls and suffer from multilingual staff shortages. Tadka turns every call into an intelligent, high-margin, sub-second order using local statistics, phonetic matching, and local neural voice synthesis — completely offline.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Deep royal maroon backdrop, gold double hairlines, and audio waveform: *"What if your restaurant phone never missed an order?"*

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Multilingual Voice Intake & Live Interruption (Barge-In)**: Real-time Hinglish parsing (*"ek dal makhani, do butter naan"*) with live English glossing.
- **Autonomous Revenue Engine**: Apriori affinity mining discovers winning pairings on the fly, offering high-margin upsells.
- **Instant Thermal Kitchen Ticket**: Automatic KOT generation and idempotent POS dispatch with sequential tracking.

## Outro / punchline
❖ TADKA: Zero cloud fees. ~40ms turn latency. 100% Local.

## User flow worth showing
Caller speaks Hinglish order → Tadka parses entities and suggests a high-margin combo → Kitchen ticket prints instantly and syncs to POS.
