2. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE (LEFT PANEL)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION TITLE:
SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

STATUS BADGE:
• PRODUCTION READY

CORE ARCHITECTURE:
A high-throughput, offline-first voice conversational runtime and statistical revenue engine engineered for sub-second restaurant phone order intake in English, Hindi, and Hinglish. Built with WebAssembly Vosk speech recognition, local Piper neural acoustic synthesis, scikit-learn intent classification, and Apriori frequent-itemset affinity mining for zero-cloud, privacy-preserving POS order dispatch.

KEY ENGINEERING HIGHLIGHTS:
- Full-Duplex Multilingual NLU Pipeline: Real-time dialect code-switching parser handling colloquial Hinglish with dual-vocabulary phonetics, homophone disambiguation, and real-time English glossing at sub-5ms parse latency.
- Autonomous Revenue & Menu Engineering Engine: Offline Apriori itemset mining (evaluating 12k+ orders in 0.25s) paired with a 4-quadrant BCG matrix (Stars, Plowhorses, Puzzles, Dogs) and empirical price elasticity guardrails to maximize average order value.
- Deterministic POS & Kitchen Dispatch Subsystem: Zero-loss idempotent order queue generating standard thermal Kitchen Order Tickets (KOT) with sequential tracking, automatic retry backoff, and live Server-Sent Events (SSE) broadcast.

ROLE:
Lead Full-Stack & AI Systems Engineer

TIMELINE:
